# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy
**Project:** Automated AI Labor - MVP
**Date:** 2026-02-10

## 1. Initial Positioning
**Value Prop:** "Get a Senior-Level Analyst Report in minutes. Don't pay if you don't use it."
**Tagline:** Your On-Demand AI Workforce.

## 2. Launch Strategy (The "Cold Start")

### Phase 1: The "Manual" Launch (Do Things That Don't Scale)
-   **Channel:** Twitter/X & LinkedIn.
-   **Tactic:** Offer "Free Custom Research Reports" to 50 targeted influencers/founders in exchange for a public review.
    -   *Why:* Real-world usage data, testimonials, refinement of "Agent Prompting".
-   **Metric:** 50 High-Quality Testimonials.

### Phase 2: Product Hunt Launch
-   **Asset:** A polished Landing Page + Demo Video.
-   **Story:** "We built an AI that does the work for you. See the live dashboard."
-   **Offer:** 50% off first 3 reports for PH community.

### Phase 3: Content Marketing (SEO)
-   **Programmatic SEO:** Generate thousands of landing pages for specific research topics.
    -   "Market research for SaaS pricing"
    -   "Competitor analysis for Fintech apps"
-   **Dogfooding:** Use our own AI to write these pages.

## 3. Pricing Model
**Result-Based Pricing (RaaS)**
Instead of a monthly subscription (SaaS), we charge per *unit of labor*.
-   **Tier 1 (Brief):** $5 / Report (Basic summary).
-   **Tier 2 (Deep Dive):** $25 / Report (Comprehensive, Multi-Source, Citations).
-   **Enterprise:** Subscription for unlimited access + API access.

## 4. Viral Loops
-   **Watermark:** Free reports have a subtle "Generated by AutoLabor.ai" watermark.
-   **Share to Unlock:** "Share your report on Twitter to unlock the next one for free."
-   **Affiliate:** 20% commission for referrals (standard SaaS playbook).

## 5. Success Metrics (First 3 Months)
-   **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** < $10.
-   **LTV (Lifetime Value):** > $50 (Target 2+ reports per user).
-   **NPS:** > 50.
